Fees & Funding

Key information about sessions, funding options and how nursery fees work.

Funding Support

We provide funded hours over the 50-week period that the nursery is open. Government funding is paid for 38 weeks of the year and we spread this funding across the weeks we are open.

15-hour universal funding

  • Two short days, 8:30am-2:30pm.
  • One full day and four additional hours, subject to spaces available.
  • Three afternoons, 1:00pm-5:00pm.

30-hour working entitlement

  • Two full days and four hours each week, or five hours if your child attends on a Monday because of the bank holiday.
  • Four short days, 8:30am-2:30pm.
  • Four afternoons, 1:00pm-5:00pm, and one additional day.

The nursery does not charge additional fees on funded hours. Any further sessions will be charged accordingly and shown as a breakdown on the nursery invoice.

It is the parent’s responsibility to update the funding code on the government childcare account every 12 weeks. If the funding code is not updated, you may lose your funding entitlement.
